## Summary

- **CONFIRMED (13):** 1a, 1b, 1c, 1d, 1e, 2a, 2b, 3, 4a, 4b, 4c, 4d, 5, 6b, 7, 9, 11 — plus 10a/10b as noted below.
- **PARTIALLY CORRECT / needs rewording (5):** 2c, 2d, 2e, 6a, 8, 10a (quote attribution), 10b (year).
- **FALSE (0)**, **UNVERIFIABLE (0)**.

The single highest-risk item is **Claim 8** (Van den Abbeele): as written it would tell a reader that a *human* microbiota produced ~20 mM acetate and ~10 mM butyrate from *non-carbohydrate substrate in general*. It was a single piglet's colonic microbiota, the substrates were specifically glutamine and glutamate, the numbers are increments over a blank, and the blank medium already contained starch and peptone. Fix before submission.

Second-highest risk is **Claim 7** (Ryder): the numbers are exact but the −50.2% floor comes from bariatric surgery. Using that span to argue heterogeneity of response to a dietary/fermentation intervention is not a fair use of the source.
